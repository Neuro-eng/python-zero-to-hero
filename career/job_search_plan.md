# Job Search Plan

## Where to look
- Company career pages (many IT services and product companies hire freshers via their own portals)
- Job portals: LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Instahyre, Wellfound (startups), Internshala (internships)
- Campus and off-campus drives, walk-ins, hackathons, local tech meetups
- Referrals: usually the highest-converting route
- Freelance and open-source: first real-world experience

## Roles to target
Python Developer (Junior), Backend Developer, Automation/QA Engineer (Python), Data Analyst, Support/Application Engineer, DevOps Trainee, Technical Support Engineer.

## Weekly targets
| Activity | Target |
|----------|--------|
| Applications (tailored) | 25-40 |
| Referral requests / networking messages | 10 |
| Mock interviews | 2 |
| Coding practice | 5 days |
| LinkedIn posts | 1-2 |
| Follow-up emails | After 5-7 days |

## Tracker (spreadsheet columns)
Company | Role | Link | Date applied | Referral? | Status | Next step | Notes

## Interview process (typical)
1. Online test (aptitude / coding)
2. Technical round (Python, SQL, project discussion)
3. HR round (communication, expectations)
4. Offer, documents, joining

## Mindset
- Rejections are normal. Review, fix, apply again.
- Each interview teaches you the market. Write down every question you couldn't answer, then learn it.
- Stay safe: **never pay money for a job offer or "training deposit"**. Genuine employers don't charge candidates.
