# 90-Day Spoken English Plan

Commit to **30 minutes daily**. Consistency beats long weekend sessions.

## Phase 1 (Days 1-30): Build the habit and the base
- Speak aloud every day. Shadow 10 minutes of one speaker (see `daily_routine.md`).
- Learn 5 new phrases a day from `phrase_bank.md`; use each in 2 sentences.
- Week 1: pronunciation basics (word stress, vowel length, "th", "v/w").
- Week 2: self-introduction in 60 seconds. Record it; re-record on Day 14.
- Week 3: describe your day, your college, your hometown, your goals (present, past, future).
- Week 4: explain a Python concept in 2 minutes (variable, loop, function).
- **Milestone:** a 2-minute self-introduction without reading.

## Phase 2 (Days 31-60): Structure and clarity
- Learn the **PREP** and **STAR** structures (see `interview_english.md`).
- Present one project in 3 minutes weekly (use `scripts/project_demo.md`).
- Reduce fillers ("umm", "like", "basically"). Replace with a silent pause.
- Join one speaking partner/club session weekly (online communities, college groups, local meetups).
- Practice stand-up updates daily (`scripts/standup_update.md`).
- **Milestone:** a 5-minute technical presentation with a clear beginning, middle and end.

## Phase 3 (Days 61-90): Pressure and polish
- Do 2 mock interviews weekly with a friend, mentor or by recording yourself.
- Answer unexpected questions: use *"That's a good question. Let me think for a second."*
- Practice Q&A: summarize the question, answer, check: *"Does that answer your question?"*
- Watch your recordings; score with `self_review_checklist.md`.
- **Milestone:** a full mock interview (HR + technical) with calm pace and clear answers.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Mon | New phrases + shadowing |
| Tue | Record 2-minute talk |
| Wed | Listen to your recording, note 3 fixes |
| Thu | Re-record with fixes |
| Fri | Present to a friend / online speaking partner |
| Sat | Mock interview / project demo |
| Sun | Light: podcast, YouTube talk, rest |
