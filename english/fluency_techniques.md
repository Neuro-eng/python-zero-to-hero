# Fluency Techniques

## 1. Fix filler words ("umm", "like", "you know")
- Record yourself and count fillers per minute.
- Replace a filler with a **silent pause**. Silence sounds thoughtful.
- Slow down slightly, since fillers appear when you speak faster than you think.
- Target: below 3 fillers per minute.

## 2. Speak in chunks, not word by word
Group words in meaning units, with tiny pauses between them:
> "I built a tracker | that records my expenses | and shows a monthly summary."

## 3. Shadowing (the most effective free method)
1. Pick a 30-second clip with a transcript.
2. Listen 3 times while reading.
3. Speak along, 1 second behind the speaker.
4. Record and compare.
Focus on rhythm and stress, not on sounding like someone else.

## 4. Word stress and sentence stress
- English is **stress-timed**: important words are loud, long and clear. Small words are quick and soft.
- "I WANT to BUILD a WEB app." (stress the content words)
- Look up stress in an online dictionary and listen to the audio every time.

## 5. Common pronunciation issues to practice
- **th**: *think, three, the, this* (tongue between teeth)
- **v / w**: *very / wary, vine / wine*
- **Final consonants**: say the "d", "t", "s" at the end: *worked, tested, tests*
- **Schwa** (the weak "uh" sound): *about, problem, computer*

## 6. Think in English
- Narrate your actions: "I'm opening the file. Now I'm running the test."
- Describe what you see for 2 minutes while walking.
- Do not translate word by word. Learn whole phrases instead.

## 7. Fix hesitation
- Have 5 "bridge phrases" ready (see `phrase_bank.md`).
- Use simple words. A clear simple sentence beats a complicated broken one.
- If you make a mistake, correct it once and continue. Don't stop.

## 8. Listening
- Daily: 15 minutes of tech podcasts or conference talks.
- Use English subtitles first, then no subtitles.
- Write down 3 phrases per day, then use them.

## 9. Vocabulary
- Learn words in sentences, not lists.
- Review with spaced repetition (Anki or similar) for 10 minutes a day.
- Learn collocations: *make a decision, run a test, fix a bug, push code, raise an issue*.

## 10. Get feedback
- Speaking partner or club once a week.
- Record weekly and compare Week 1 vs Week 4.
- Ask one mentor to point out your 2 most common mistakes.

## Free resource types to look for
Tech conference talks, TED talks, BBC Learning English, podcasts on software/careers, YouTube channels about interview English, your own recordings. Choose speakers you find clear and easy to follow.
