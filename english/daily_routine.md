# 30-Minute Daily Routine

| Min | Activity | How |
|-----|----------|-----|
| 0-10 | **Shadowing** | Play 20-30 seconds of a clear speaker (TED-style talk, tech talk, podcast). Repeat after them at the same speed, copying rhythm, stress and intonation. Replay the same clip 5 times. |
| 10-15 | **Phrase practice** | Take 5 phrases from the phrase bank. Say each in 2 situations from your own life/work. |
| 15-25 | **Speak and record** | Pick a topic. Speak for 2 minutes without stopping. Record on your phone. If you freeze, say "What I mean is..." and continue. |
| 25-30 | **Review** | Listen once. Write 1 thing you did well, 1 thing to fix. Log it with `speaking_tracker.py`. |

## Topic ideas (pick one a day)
- Explain what a variable/function/API is, as if to a friend.
- Describe your last project and what was hard.
- Why do you want to work in IT?
- Describe a bug you fixed.
- Explain your daily routine / your city / your favorite app.
- Summarize a video you watched today.

## Immersion habits (no extra time)
- Change phone and laptop language to English.
- Watch tech talks with English subtitles, then without.
- Think in English while walking: narrate what you see.
- Keep a notebook of new words with a sentence you made yourself.
