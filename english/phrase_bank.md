# Phrase Bank for Meetings and Presentations

## Starting
- Good morning/afternoon, everyone. Thanks for joining.
- Let me start by introducing myself.
- Today I'd like to walk you through...
- The purpose of this session is to...
- By the end, you'll understand...

## Structuring
- First of all... / Secondly... / Finally...
- Let's move on to...
- That brings me to my next point.
- To give you some background...
- Let me go back to what I said earlier.

## Explaining
- In simple terms, this means...
- For example... / For instance...
- Let me break it down into steps.
- The main reason is...
- What this shows is...
- Think of it like...

## Describing data and trends
- Sales increased by 20% compared to last month.
- There was a sharp rise/drop in...
- The number remained stable/flat.
- The biggest factor was...
- As you can see on this chart...

## Giving opinions politely
- In my opinion... / From my experience...
- I would suggest we...
- One option could be...
- I see your point, but I'd like to add...
- I may be wrong, but I think...

## Buying time (instead of "umm")
- That's a good question. Let me think for a second.
- Let me put it another way.
- What I mean is...
- Give me a moment to check that.

## Asking for clarification
- Could you repeat that, please?
- Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you say it again more slowly?
- Just to confirm, you mean...?
- Could you give an example?

## Disagreeing respectfully
- I understand your concern, however...
- That's a fair point. Another way to look at it is...
- I see it slightly differently...

## Updates at work
- I completed ___ yesterday. Today I'm working on ___. I'm blocked by ___.
- I need some help with ___.
- It will be done by end of day / Friday.
- I ran into an issue with ___, and here's how I'm fixing it.
- Let me share my screen.

## Closing
- To sum up... / To wrap up...
- The key takeaway is...
- The next steps are...
- Thank you for your time. Any questions?

## Email openers and closers
- I hope you're doing well. / Thank you for your quick reply.
- I'm writing to follow up on...
- Please find attached... / Could you please confirm...
- Looking forward to hearing from you. / Best regards,
