# How to Present in English (Fluent and Clear)

## 1. The structure: "Tell them what you'll tell them"

| Part | Time share | What to say |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Opening** | 10% | Greet, introduce yourself, topic, why it matters, agenda |
| **Body** | 75% | 2-3 main points, each with an example or data |
| **Closing** | 15% | Summary, key takeaway, next step, thank you, invite questions |

**Opening template**
> "Good morning, everyone. My name is ___. Today I'll talk about ___. This matters because ___. I'll cover three things: first ___, second ___, and finally ___."

**Closing template**
> "To summarize: ___, ___, and ___. The key takeaway is ___. Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions."

## 2. Use PREP for every point
**P**oint, **R**eason, **E**xample, **P**oint again.
> "We should add tests *(point)* because they catch bugs early *(reason)*. For example, last week a test caught a crash in my login function *(example)*. So, testing saves time *(point)*."

## 3. Slide rules
- One idea per slide, at most 6 lines, large font.
- Slide title = the message ("Sales grew 30% in Q4"), not a topic ("Sales").
- Visuals over text. Do not read slides word for word.
- 1 slide per 1-2 minutes.

## 4. Delivery: the 5 controls
1. **Pace:** about 120-150 words per minute. Slower for key points.
2. **Pause:** stop for 1-2 seconds after each important sentence. Silence sounds confident.
3. **Stress:** emphasize key words ("sales GREW thirty percent").
4. **Intonation:** voice goes down at the end of statements, up for yes/no questions.
5. **Body language:** eye contact (or look at the camera), open posture, calm hands.

## 5. Online presentations
- Camera at eye level, good light, quiet room, mic test.
- Share only the needed window, close notifications.
- Check in: *"Can everyone see my screen?"* and *"Any questions so far?"*

## 6. Handling Q&A
1. Listen fully. Do not interrupt.
2. Paraphrase: *"So you're asking whether..."*
3. Answer in 2-3 sentences using PREP.
4. Check: *"Does that answer your question?"*
5. If you don't know: *"I don't know that yet, but I'll check and get back to you by tomorrow."*

## 7. Handling nerves
- Practice the first 30 seconds until it is automatic.
- Breathe in for 4 seconds, out for 6, before you start.
- It is OK to pause and look at your notes. Say: *"Let me check my notes for a second."*
- Nervousness is normal. Listeners notice it far less than you think.

## 8. Rehearsal plan
1. Write bullet points, not a full script.
2. Speak it aloud alone, 3 times.
3. Time it, and cut 10%.
4. Record, review, fix.
5. Present to one friend and ask for 2 honest comments.
