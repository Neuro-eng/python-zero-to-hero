# Script: Self-Introduction (60 seconds)

Fill the blanks, practice aloud 10 times, then speak without reading.

> Hello, my name is **___**. I'm from **___**, and I recently completed **___** (degree/course) at **___**.
>
> I'm passionate about **Python and problem-solving**. In the last **___** months, I've built projects like **___** and **___**, and I've shared them on GitHub.
>
> My main strengths are **___** and **___**. For example, **(one short example)**.
>
> I'm looking for an opportunity as a **___**, where I can **learn from experienced engineers and add value to the team**.
>
> Thank you.

**Checklist:** name, background, skills, proof (projects), goal. About 130 words at a calm pace.
