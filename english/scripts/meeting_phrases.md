# Script: Speaking Up in Meetings

**Join the conversation**
- "Can I add something here?"
- "If I may, I'd like to share a quick thought."

**Ask for clarity**
- "Just to be sure I understand, you want ___, correct?"
- "Could you share an example?"

**Suggest an idea**
- "What if we try ___?"
- "One option could be ___. The benefit is ___."

**Give a status**
- "I'm on track to deliver by Friday."
- "I'd need two more days because ___."

**Close a topic**
- "So, to summarize, we agreed on ___. I'll take the action on ___ by ___."
